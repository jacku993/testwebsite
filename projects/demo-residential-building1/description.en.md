## Project description

This is a sample project description. The text should describe the project in a neutral way and clearly state the actual scope of work delivered by the company.

## Communication principle

We do not claim full responsibility for the entire investment if the company delivered only a selected scope of work. Instead, we use wording such as:

- scope of work delivered by our company,
- participation in the project,
- contractor for a selected scope,
- works delivered as part of the investment.
